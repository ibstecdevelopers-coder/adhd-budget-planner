📱 Install on iPhone / iPad

  1. Open your planner link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from the new icon — it now works offline too.

🤖 Install on Android

  1. Open your planner link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open it from your home screen — it now works offline too.

🧭 Your first 10 minutes

  1. Open Start here inside the app — it’s a 6-step checklist.
  2. Pick your currency in Settings.
  3. Add your paydays and regular bills on the Calendar and set them to repeat.
  4. Type a monthly amount for each category on Budget.
  5. Log one purchase. Done — that’s the habit.

💾 Keep your data safe

Your planner is saved privately in the browser you use — there’s no account and no bank link.

  1. Every few weeks: Settings → Download backup.
  2. New phone or computer? Open the planner there, then Settings → Restore from backup.
  3. Clearing your browser’s site data erases the planner — back up first.
  4. Each device keeps its own copy; use backup/restore to move between them.

✨ Little habits that make it stick

• Check Today once a day — it tells you the one thing to do.

• Log purchases right away with the + Log button.

• Tick bills on the calendar when paid — the rest updates itself.

• Use Can I afford it? before impulse buys.

• Mark no-spend days — streaks are motivating.

• End of month: read the Month page and jot a note.
