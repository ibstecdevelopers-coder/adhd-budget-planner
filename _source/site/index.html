Today

One number you can trust: safe to spend.

Income minus what you’ve spent minus the bills that are still coming. Then split into a daily amount — so “can I grab coffee?” finally has an answer.

  • Safe-to-spend and safe-per-day, updated as you go
  • “One thing” prompt — the next bill, or a gentle nudge
  • “Can I afford it?” box — no mental math
  • Savings, debt and goals in one glance
Paycheck plan

Give every paycheck a job.

Paid weekly, every two weeks or monthly? Clear Cents lines your bills up against the paycheck you’ll have before each one is due — and shows what’s left from each check.

  • Bills split between paychecks automatically
  • See if one paycheck is stretched too thin
  • Works with any pay schedule
Calendar

Bills and paydays that never sneak up.

Add rent, subscriptions and paydays once and set them to repeat. Tick one off and it’s recorded as a payment everywhere else — no double entry.

  • Weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or yearly repeats
  • Overdue items flagged on your Today page
  • Reminders for things without a price (renewals, birthdays)
Monthly dashboard

See where your money actually went.

Pick any month for income vs. spending, a category chart, needs vs. wants, budget vs. actual and a notes box for what you learned.

  • Spending by category donut chart
  • Over-budget categories called out kindly
  • Searchable spending log with CSV export
Debt payoff

A real date for debt-free.

Choose highest-interest-first or smallest-balance-first, add any extra you can afford, and watch the payoff dates move. It even shows how much interest you save versus minimums only.

  • Payoff month for every debt
  • Interest saved and months saved
  • Warns when a minimum doesn’t cover the interest
Savings & goals

Save for what matters most.

Make a fund for anything — emergency cushion, a trip, a new laptop — and see the monthly amount needed to reach it by your date. Goals live alongside with tap-to-update status.

  • Unlimited savings funds with target dates
  • Scheduled transfers fill funds automatically
  • Goals: not started → in progress → done

Questions

Good to know.

Is this a Google Sheets or Excel template?

No. Clear Cents is a web app — it runs in your browser, so there are no formulas to break and nothing else to buy.

Where is my data stored? Do I connect my bank?

Your numbers are saved only in the browser on your device. There’s no account and no bank connection. You can download a backup file any time and restore it on another device.

Does it work on my phone? What about offline?

Yes. Open your link on iPhone or Android and add it to your home screen — it then opens like an app and works without internet. On a computer you can also use the downloadable offline HTML file.

Will my phone and laptop sync?

Each device keeps its own copy (that’s what keeps it private). To move your planner, tap Download backup on one device and Restore on the other.

Which currencies are supported?

15 including USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD, NZD, INR, PKR, AED, SGD, ZAR, JPY, MXN, PHP and NGN.

Is it really for ADHD?

It’s designed to cut the friction that makes budgeting hard for ADHD brains: one clear number, one next step, 5-second logging, repeating bills you set once, and visual progress instead of rows of numbers. Anyone who wants a simpler budget will like it too.

Is there a subscription?

No. One purchase, lifetime access to your planner.
